# Evidence Current

A white field gives every object a clear purpose. The composition moves as a single current through generous space, with deep blue carrying direction and near-black grounding the reading order. Meticulously crafted alignments and restrained details make the structure feel deliberate rather than decorated.

Illustrations are the anchors. Their dimensional material creates an approachable contrast with precise paths and small technical annotations. Scale communicates the change from an unstructured request to a focused practice, then to an artifact someone can inspect. Painstaking attention to spacing keeps those transitions visible before any label is read.

Typography has two voices: a substantial, human headline and quiet, exact supporting labels. Short phrases sit beside their objects rather than competing with them. Hierarchy comes from proportion and distance, the product of deep expertise rather than a collection of panels.

Blue routes connect discrete stages without turning the page into a wall of containers. A controlled rhythm of numbered stops lets the eye follow a practical sequence while the open background leaves room for interpretation. Master-level execution means each connection has a clear beginning and end.

The closing evidence artifact is tangible and modest. Small marks suggest inspection and prioritization without pretending that a diagram proves an outcome. Final refinement removes visual noise, sharpens contrast and balances the remaining forms until every detail belongs.
